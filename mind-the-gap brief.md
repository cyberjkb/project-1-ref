Project 1: "Mind the Gap Man" – Superhero Services for Hire (Parody Persona)

• External User's Goal: The user (a distressed London citizen) seeks clear, easy-to-understand information on hiring a local, budget-friendly superhero to solve everyday crises—ranging from rescuing cats and fixing impossible printer jams to holding the Northern Line doors open.
• Site Owner's Goal: The site owner wants to create a lighthearted, visually striking parody webpage that outlines heroic services, response times, and pricing tiers using structured HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap for an intuitive, comic-book-inspired user experience.

3-Page Website Structure
1. Home Page (index.html): Features a dramatic "Hero Alert" banner, the superhero's origin story, live crime/inconvenience stats, and citizen testimonials. Purpose: Entertain the visitor and establish the parody persona immediately.
2. Heroic Services & Pricing Page (services.html): Breaks down hireable packages (e.g., Bronze Tier: Spider Removal & Jar Opening, Silver Tier: Queue Jumping & Wi-Fi Fixing, Gold Tier: Full Arch-Nemesis Defeat). Purpose: Clearly present service tiers and features using structured comparisons.
3. Distress Signal / Booking Page (contact.html): An emergency dispatch form where citizens can log their crisis level, coordinates, and preferred superhero arrival method. Purpose: Provide an interactive call-to-action to book a mission.

Potential Features Might Include
• Hero Section: A bold Bootstrap header/Jumbotron with a dramatic superhero slogan, cape imagery, and an "Emergency Dispatch" button.
• Service Pricing Cards: Use Bootstrap's card components and 3-column grid system to compare heroic tiers and pricing side-by-side.
• List of Superpowers & Weaknesses: Styled using Bootstrap's list group component to humorously outline what the hero will and won't do (e.g., "Will not fight south of Zone 4 after 9 PM").
• Distress Signal Form: A reporting form styled with Bootstrap's form components, dropdowns, and danger/warning button classes (btn-danger).
